Privacy Policy

Hindsight Atlas Uploader · Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Hindsight Atlas Uploader (the "App") accesses, uses, stores and protects information when you sign in with your Google Account. The App is operated by the owner of the Hindsight Atlas YouTube channel (the "Operator"), who can be reached at rehobotlabs07@gmail.com. The App's home page is https://eliasfire617.github.io/hindsight-atlas-uploader/.

1. What the App does

The App is a private publishing tool. It takes video files, titles, descriptions and thumbnail images that the Operator has produced, and uploads them to the Operator's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.

2. Google user data the App accesses

The App requests a single Google OAuth permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: permission to upload videos and their thumbnails to the YouTube channel of the account that signs in.

With this permission the App sends data to YouTube; it does not read the channel's existing videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, contacts, email, or any other Google data. Google returns an OAuth access token and refresh token to the App, and YouTube returns the ID of each uploaded video.

3. How the data is used

  • The OAuth tokens are used only to authenticate upload requests to the YouTube Data API.
  • The video ID returned by YouTube is used only to attach the thumbnail and to keep a local record of what was uploaded.

Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, training of machine-learning models, or any purpose other than the upload function described above.

4. Storage and security

The refresh token is stored in a single file on the Operator's own computer, readable only by the Operator's user account (file permission 600). The OAuth client secret is stored the same way. No Google user data is stored on any server, cloud database or third-party service operated by the App. All communication with Google uses HTTPS.

5. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party. It is transferred only to Google itself, through the YouTube Data API, in order to perform the upload.

6. Retention and deletion

The refresh token is kept until the Operator signs out or revokes access. You can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; once revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the Operator's computer. Videos uploaded to YouTube remain on YouTube under YouTube's own terms and can be deleted from YouTube Studio. To request deletion of any data held by the App, write to the contact address below.

7. Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App also follows the YouTube API Services Terms of Service, and users of the App are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact
